Storage

How should I store jasmine pearls?

Keep them in an airtight container in a cool, dark, dry place, away from strong odors such as spices or coffee. Tea readily absorbs smells.

How long does it stay fresh?

Jasmine green tea tastes best while its fragrance is lively, so enjoy it within a few months of opening. Check the pouch for its best-by date.

Brewing

What water temperature should I use?

About 80°C (175°F). Boiling water can make green tea taste bitter, so let the kettle rest a few minutes after it boils.

How many pearls per cup?

About a teaspoon, roughly 5–6 pearls, for an 8 oz (240 ml) cup. Steep 2–3 minutes.

Can I re-steep the pearls?

Yes. The pearls can be infused several times; add a little extra steeping time to each round.
