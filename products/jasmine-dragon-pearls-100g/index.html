Tasting notes

Floral: a clear jasmine aroma that greets you before the first sip.

Sweet: a gentle, honeyed sweetness from tender green tea leaves.

Smooth: soft and rounded, with very little bitterness when brewed at the right temperature.

Brewing guide

Brewing guide
WaterAbout 80°C / 175°F (not boiling)
AmountAbout 1 tsp, or 5–6 pearls per cup (8 oz / 240 ml)
Steep2–3 minutes
InfusionsSeveral; add 30 seconds or so to each new steep

Tip: rinse your cup with warm water first, and try a glass vessel to watch the pearls open.

Keeping it fresh

Store in an airtight container away from light, heat, moisture and strong odors. Jasmine tea readily absorbs other aromas, so keep it away from spices and coffee.
